why local-first

ISP lines drop in the 6am rush, and waiting on a cloud webhook strands people outside. So the scan is verified against a local SQLite database, the door opens from that answer, and the cloud only hears about it later through a sync queue. The demo on the home page runs a real SQLite in your browser to show the shape of it.
