why local-first

Autonomous Hardware Fast-Path: Gyms face severe morning ISP dropouts. If a turnstile depends on an external API or cloud webhook, dozens of members are stranded outside at 6:00 AM. In this architecture, biometric template verification (1:N DSP minutiae matching) and the 5,000ms dry-contact relay pulse occur entirely on-board the BioMax terminal hardware in under 15 milliseconds. Neither the local host PC nor the cloud is in the critical path to open the door.

Win32 Unmanaged Interop (.NET 8 x86): The commercial biometric SDK (FK623Attend.dll) is an unmanaged 32-bit Win32 library communicating over raw TCP binary sockets on Port 5005. Rather than tolerating out-of-process IPC overhead, the edge background daemon compiles strictly to .NET 8 x86 (win-x86) with high-performance P/Invoke interop and synchronized socket pools managed by BiomaxGlobalLock.

Transactional Outbox & Biometric Quarantine: When internet connectivity drops, the gym operates at 100% autonomy—turnstile scans, POS registrations, and Day Book drawer accounting continue offline. A Transactional Outbox pattern (SyncOutboxEvents) batches attendance records with exponential backoff. In strict compliance with DPDP and GDPR, the sync worker permanently blocks biometric templates and hardware NVRAM dumps from syncing to the cloud: biometrics stay quarantined on the gym LAN, backed up solely to an on-premise encrypted SQLite vault for instant fleet recovery.
